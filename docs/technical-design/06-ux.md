# UX Design

Each role opens the app with a different question. The landing page should
answer it without a click.

| Role | Opens the app asking | Landing page answers it with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Employee | "How much did I make, and how many vacation days do I have?" | Insight cards, leave balances, recent slips |
| Manager | "Is anyone waiting on me, and who is away?" | Pending count, away this month, then the queue |
| Bookkeeper | "What is unfinished?" | Time-off to approve, unpublished periods |

Navigation is role-scoped. An employee never sees Manager items. A
bookkeeper never sees the personal employee group (they are not on a
client payroll). Combined with layout guards and RLS, the UI does not
show a door that is locked.

The chrome is English and left-to-right. Empty states name the next step.

## 1. Employee

Personal nav: Dashboard, Pay slips, Documents, Time off.

### Dashboard (`/employee`)

Latest net (vs previous month), 12-month average, deduction rate,
vacation remaining, a net-pay trend, recent slips, recent files.
Incoming **team-join** cards sit at the top when a manager has asked
them to report to them — accept or decline here.

No pay slips: the empty copy says the first slip appears when the
bookkeeper publishes, and leave balances still show.

### Pay slips (`/employee/payslips`)

Published months with amounts and an open link. There is no
`/employee/payslips/[id]` breakdown page yet.

### Documents (`/employee/documents`)

Folders by kind (only kinds that have files). Form 101: link to the
external form, then upload the PDF (`/employee/documents/form-101`).

### Time off (`/employee/time-off`)

Balance cards, then the request form (calendar days vs working days, and
remaining after submit). A remaining-day option exists for types that
track a balance. Pending rows have Cancel.

## 2. Manager

Team nav: Team overview, Approvals, Team, Shared files, Calendar.
The personal employee nav stays above that.

### Team overview (`/manager`)

Waiting on you, direct reports, away this month, team vacation/sick left,
then a short pending list.

### Approvals (`/manager/approvals`)

Cards: name, type, dates, working days, remaining if approved, overlap
warning, reason, attachments, Approve / Reject. Reject asks for a note.
Any manager at the business can decide, not only the line manager. You
cannot decide your own request.

### Team (`/manager/team`)

Direct reports: last net, average, vacation, sick, pending. Asking
someone onto the team sends a request they must accept. Waiting asks
are listed. On a person page the manager can **remove them from the
team** (they stay at the company). That is the only manager write on
people besides the ask.

### Shared files (`/manager/shared`)

Documents and slips with `visible_to_managers`.

### Calendar (`/manager/calendar`)

Month grid. Green = approved leave, amber = pending. Click a day for
who, leave type, written reason, and the date range. Unscheduled
remaining-day requests are not painted. `?month=YYYY-MM` for prev/next.

## 3. Bookkeeper

Nav: What needs attention, Approvals, Businesses, Payroll periods,
Documents.

### Queue (`/bookkeeper`)

Unpublished periods and leave waiting. Approvals here cover every client
company, including people with no line manager.

### Businesses (`/bookkeeper/businesses`)

Client list. Create a business, then on the business page: people,
one-time invites, **reusable employee link and reusable manager link**
(separate, so an employee cannot join as a manager), uploads, and at the
bottom remove-this-business. Person page: slips, documents, terminate,
assign a line manager directly.

People are not a top-level `/bookkeeper/employees` app.

### Periods (`/bookkeeper/periods` and `[periodId]`)

Open a month, bulk-upload PDFs (parse + suggested employee), assign,
then Publish. Publish stays disabled while files are unassigned, and
confirms that employees will see the slips.

### Documents (`/bookkeeper/documents`)

Firm-wide cabinet. Empty = dashed empty state. With files = collapsible
folders per kind (Form 106, Form 101, Contracts, Pension, Other). Pay
slips stay on Payroll periods.

## 4. Cross-cutting

**Loading** — no `loading.tsx` skeletons yet.

**Errors** — field-level for validation, inline on cards for actions.

**Mobile** — employee pages stack. Bookkeeper bulk upload is
desktop-first.

**Confirmation** — reserved for publish, terminate, and delete-business.
Team remove is also explicit because it changes reporting lines.

## 5. Trade-offs in v1

- **No email or in-app notifications.** A manager learns about a request
  by opening the app.
- **No delegation** when the only manager is on leave — but a
  *colleague* manager or the bookkeeper can still decide.
- **PDF parse suggests; humans confirm.** A mis-assigned salary is worse
  than a slower click.
- **No bulk approve.** Twenty requests are twenty clicks.
- **No automated accrual or year-end carryover.**
- **No automated RLS/e2e suite in the repo yet.**
- **UI is not RTL**, even though many users are Hebrew-speaking.
